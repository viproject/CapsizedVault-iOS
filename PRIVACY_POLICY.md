# Privacy Policy

**CapsizedVault**  
Last updated: August 2026

---

## Overview

Capsized Vault does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All wallet data is stored locally on your device and never leaves it, except as described below.

---

## What data leaves your device

### Monero node connection
To sync your wallet, the app connects to a Monero node. Your IP address is visible to the node operator. No wallet addresses, balances, or transaction data are sent to ViProject Ltd or any third party controlled by us — they are transmitted directly between your device and the node.

### XMR price
The app fetches the current Monero (XMR) exchange rate from [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com) (`api.coingecko.com`). This request contains no personal data — only the coin identifier and requested fiat currencies.

### Version check
On launch, the app checks `https://capsized.io/version.json` to determine whether an update is available. This request contains no personal data.

---

## What stays on your device

| Data | Storage |
|------|---------|
| Wallet files (encrypted) | Local app storage |
| Wallet passwords | iOS Keychain |
| PIN / biometric credentials | iOS Keychain (PIN stored as SHA-256 hash only) |
| Transaction history | Local app database |
| XMR price cache | Local app database |
| Selected nodes | Local app database |

None of the above is transmitted to ViProject Ltd or any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, and no advertising frameworks.

---

## Camera

The camera is used exclusively to scan QR codes for Monero wallet addresses. Images are never stored or transmitted.

---

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

---

## Seed phrases and private keys

Seed phrases and private keys entered into the app (e.g. during wallet restore) are processed entirely on-device to derive your wallet. They are never transmitted to ViProject Ltd or any third party.

---

Since Capsized Vault does not collect any personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our end. If you have any privacy-related questions, please contact us at the address below.
